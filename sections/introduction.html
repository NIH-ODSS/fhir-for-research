Introduction to FHIR

Understand FHIR before putting it to work.

FHIR® (Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources, pronounced “fire”) is an HL7 standard for representing and exchanging health data. Learn how its resources, terminology, and APIs fit together, and what they mean for research.
