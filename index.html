FHIR for Research

A guide for health researchers

Where does FHIR fit in your research?

Learn how FHIR can help you access electronic health record data, bring new tools into clinical workflows, and make research data easier to reuse.

HL7® FHIR® (Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources, pronounced “fire”) is a standard for representing and exchanging health data. FHIR, when combined with other metadata standards like NIH Common Data Elements, can standardize terminologies used in data exchange. These capabilities can support FAIR data practices: making data findable, accessible, interoperable, and reusable.

This guide explains the concepts and technologies, with examples and workshops to help you apply them in your own work.

What can FHIR do to support research?

Bring research innovations into clinical workflows

Use SMART on FHIR to integrate research apps with EHRs, CDS Hooks to deliver clinical decision support during care, and Clinical Quality Language (CQL) to express the underlying logic.

Automate EHR data collection

Pull available clinical data directly from EHRs into REDCap using FHIR, reducing manual data entry for chart abstraction.

Access data for a study population

FHIR Bulk Data lets you retrieve data for a patient population in a consistent, structured format.
